Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

Effective: October 24, 2026. Applies to shared accounts for rilakbest.com and rilaksekai.com.

1. Registration and sign-in

Continuing with Google accepts these Terms and Privacy Policy. Your first Google sign-in automatically creates an account with a random username and signs you in immediately. You may change that generated username once in account settings. Invalid or duplicate names do not use up this change. Setting a password is optional.

Registration and sign-in using a username and password remain available. Use only accounts you own. Do not use another person’s credentials or interfere with the service.

2. Linking Google and switching to an existing account

On an existing account, select Link Google account and verify your current password. This verification lasts only for the current login session and ends on sign-out or session expiry. The entered password is not kept in browser storage.

Before setting a password, an automatically created Google account can switch to an existing account by verifying that account’s username or email and password. Matching email addresses never automatically merge accounts. An existing account already linked to another Google account cannot be selected.

The existing account retains its username, saved data and permissions, and future Google sign-ins use that account. Sessions for the automatically created account are ended. Its data is kept as a backup without overwriting or merging it into the existing account. The linked account can download this backup in settings. A working password is required before unlinking Google.

Data not yet saved to the server is backed up separately in the browser used for linking and included when downloading the backup from that browser. This local backup is not transferred to other devices and is removed when browser storage is cleared.

3. Information collected and its purpose

  • Shared account: Username, optional email, a one-way hash of any password you set, and consent records support sign-in and account management.
  • Google authentication: Google sign-in may temporarily deliver an authentication token containing a name, email and profile picture, and Google displays its information-sharing notice. After verification, we store only a one-way SHA-256 hash of the account identifier for sign-in and linking. We do not store the name, email, profile picture or original identifier received from Google, or automatically copy the Google email into the recovery address. Users may separately register an optional recovery email. We do not collect your Google password or access Gmail messages or Google Drive files. Google credentials are used for verification and are not stored in persistent browser storage.
  • Saving and synchronization: Saved calculations, site settings, friend codes you choose to save, and backups retained when linking an existing account.
  • Security: Session and verification records, IP addresses, browser information, referrers and request records help prevent abuse and diagnose errors.

4. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies maintain your login and bind Google verification requests. Browser storage may contain account display information, preferences and local saved data. Signing out ends the current session and its password verification for Google linking. Local saved data may remain separately from server data.

5. External services

Google provides Google authentication. Cloudflare provides service delivery, server storage and security. Google’s privacy policy applies to information Google processes during authentication. Account and backup data are used for the service purposes described here and are not sold.

6. Retention, unlinking and deletion requests

Account information and server data are retained while you use the service. Backups from a previous Google account remain associated with the linked existing account. Unlinking removes the Google identifier hash, while a separately registered recovery email and saved data remain.

Available account settings allow profile changes, unlinking and backup downloads. You may request account or backup deletion from the site operator; account deletion includes linked-account backups. Data cannot be recovered after deletion is processed. Security records may be retained as needed for abuse prevention and operation.
